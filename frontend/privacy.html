Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026 • Application: YT-1M Automation Studio
Section 01

Introduction & Purpose

Welcome to YT-1M Automation Studio ("we," "our," or "the Application"). We are dedicated to providing powerful, automated procedural audio-visual synthesis tools for creators. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Application and the choices you have associated with that data.

YT-1M Automation Studio operates locally or through a user-hosted control center, allowing content creators to synthesize relaxation binaural audio, render atmospheric 1080p nature video loops, and automatically schedule publication to their authenticated YouTube channels.

Section 02 • Google Verification Compliance

YouTube API Services & Google User Data

YT-1M Automation Studio uses YouTube API Services to facilitate video uploads and retrieve channel identity. By using our Application to connect your YouTube account, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • You are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • Your use of Google services is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
Google API Services User Data Policy:
YT-1M Automation Studio’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
Section 03

Information We Collect and Access

We strictly limit our access to data that is directly required to perform video rendering and YouTube publication:

  • OAuth Authentication Tokens: When you connect your Google Account via OAuth 2.0, we receive an access token and refresh token that allow our application to publish videos to your specified channel on your behalf.
  • Basic Channel Metadata: We retrieve your public YouTube Channel Title, Channel Handle (e.g., @mychannel), Channel ID, subscriber count, and profile picture avatar strictly to display your connected channels within your local dashboard.
  • Automated Render Logs: We maintain local logs of generated videos, durations, and publication timestamps on your local machine to keep your upload counters accurate.
  • Gmail Address (Optional): If you configure Gmail delivery alerts, your email address is used solely to dispatch video upload confirmation receipts.
We DO NOT collect or access: Your private YouTube watch history, comments, personal search queries, payment data, or any other personal Google Account information outside the explicit scopes authorized.
Section 04

How We Use Your Information

The data we process is used exclusively for the following functionality:

  • Uploading Videos: Using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope, the Application uploads your rendered 1080p nature soundscape videos to your authorized channel.
  • Displaying Connected Channels: Using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly scope, we display your channel's public title and avatar so you can switch between multiple channels.
  • Automated Disk Maintenance: All temporary intermediate clips, WAV audio files, and MP4 master files are permanently purged from server disk storage immediately after confirmed upload.
Section 05 • Security & Revocation

Data Security, Storage & How to Revoke Access

We believe in complete transparency and full user autonomy over data:

  • Local Storage: Your OAuth tokens and settings are saved locally in your application directory (backend/tokens/) on your own machine. We do not transmit your credentials to any external third-party server.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data, channel data, or Google user data to anyone under any circumstances.
How to Revoke Access at Any Time:
You have the complete right to revoke YT-1M Automation Studio’s access to your Google Account at any time. You can revoke access directly via the Google Security Settings & Permissions Page. Once revoked, the application can no longer upload videos or read your channel data.

Additionally, you can click "Delete Channel" in our dashboard at any time to instantly delete your local tokens and settings from disk.

Section 06

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our services are not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Furthermore, all videos generated and uploaded by the application are set with selfDeclaredMadeForKids: false to comply with YouTube COPPA policies.
